# FlexWeek 1.5.0 release notes

Places homework around school and sports. Download, open a window, create an account.

Already using FlexWeek? Choose Check for updates in Settings, or use Update now
when the app offers it. Your account, schedule, saved looks, and paired devices
stay in place.

## What changed

### One desktop style: Material 3
- The desktop app now has one style, Material 3. Every start opens it, including
  for anyone who had picked another style. Setup starts at Your week.
- The Widgets styles and their designs (Today's app, Timeline, Mission control,
  Bento, Retro desktop, Clay deck, Day dial and One thing) can no longer be
  chosen, and the switch between them and Material 3 is gone. The Ctrl+K command
  bar and the older focus screen are gone too.
- Help, About, Update, the Sign out question, Manage account and Copies of this
  week now open as Material sheets.
- If FlexWeek cannot draw with the graphics card, it saves unsaved edits and
  starts again drawing on the CPU. It says so once instead of switching to
  another style. If that fails too, a page says what happened and offers Quit
  and Try again.
- When FlexWeek cannot draw its window at all, the error page can offer a newer
  version with Update now. It uses the same download and install as the update
  sheet.

### Weekly fixed times, Undo and reminders
- A fixed time can repeat every week. Turn on Repeats every week in Add fixed
  time or Edit fixed time, and choose an optional Until date. Changing one asks
  This week only or This week and every week after. The days hint now says
  "Pick every day it happens."
- An account can hold up to 200 repeating blocks, counting School, activities
  and weekly fixed times. The old limit was School and eight activities.
- Saving a weekly fixed time from this week on, or deleting School or an
  activity this week and later, can be undone. The notice says that later
  changes are undone with it.
- Reminders stay on screen as a card with Snooze and Close when your computer
  shows no pop-ups, or when Leave reminders on screen is on.

### Settings, Setup and the rest of the desktop
- Material Settings now covers the settings the former styles had. Alerts has
  the reminder sound and tone with a test play, a Spotify link, volume, the end
  chime, tray notices, letting reminders through Do not disturb, and muting
  alerts while a focus session runs. It also shows the reminder limits and the
  Spotify block-start note, and the reminder time accepts any minute from 0 to
  120.
- Settings > Focus offers Short, Standard, Long or Custom timer lengths, with a
  slider for each length under Custom. It also has splitting long homework into
  focus sessions and marking homework finished when its session ends. The old
  single 25/5 switch is gone; a saved 25/5 timer shows as Custom.
- Settings > Planning has Study hours and School hours, which you can change at
  any time. Open on chooses Week, Day, Month or My day as the screen FlexWeek
  starts on after you sign in. Planning style offers plan it for me, suggest a
  time, or I'll drag it. When you place homework by hand, Plan's button reads
  Suggest times.
- Settings > Look has an accent colour on top of the preset (Same as preset by
  default), a Sans, Serif or Mono font choice, and Animations: Off, Reduce,
  Normal or More. Animations replaces the Reduce motion switch, so a saved More
  or Off is no longer overwritten. High contrast, Paper, Ink and Terminal are
  now Material colour presets.
- Settings > This computer lists each linked phone with when it last synced and
  a Remove button that asks first. It also has Sync now, the sync status, the
  clock warning, the sync problem line and the relay note.
- Alarms can be added, edited and deleted under Settings > Alerts. When one
  rings, a Material screen offers Dismiss and Snooze.
- Add asks Add homework or Add fixed time. A failed save offers Retry save in
  the top bar and in the message. Ctrl+Shift+L opens the week as a list.
- Ctrl+N starts a new homework and F1 opens Help. Ctrl+C, Ctrl+D and Delete
  copy, duplicate and delete the block that has keyboard focus. Delete asks
  first and can be undone. None of these keys acts while you are typing.
- More > Planning has Copy day, Copy last week's fixed times, Reload this week,
  Copies of this week and, when you have set a link, Open Spotify link.
- Setup is three steps of multiple-choice cards: School hours, Study hours, and
  Look & alerts. Your week is drawn beside them as you pick. Cards cover the
  usual school days, when homework may be planned, how long before things start
  to remind you, the colour preset, light or dark, and row size.
- Setup's detailed controls for sports and jobs, the clock, the week start, the
  evening cutoff, the drag step, hours for each day, the alarm sound and a
  Spotify link appear only after a card that needs them, such as "Different
  times, sports, or a job…". The preview shows only what you have picked.
- Setup no longer asks for a first homework or ends on a summary page. The last
  step has Open my week. Setup left on one of the removed pages resumes on Look
  & alerts. Its default school hours are now Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 3:00
  PM (they were 8:00 AM to 2:30 PM); a new School made in Settings uses the same
  hours.
- Settings > Look > Advanced settings has a Simple, More and All switch. Simple
  holds the preset, mode, corner shape, block style, row size and text size.
  More adds the layout and grid dials; All adds the colour codes and readability
  check. Switching never changes a value. The choice stays on this computer and
  starts on All after a setup where you opened the detailed controls.
- Deleting one day of School or an activity, and the question about edited
  weeks, now use choice cards. The choice you can undo is picked first; each
  card says what it keeps and whether it can be undone. Delete or Save confirms
  it. Up and Down move the pick, Enter confirms and Esc cancels.
- The bottom notice now says what Copy, Copy day, Undo, Redo, Reload this week,
  a refused action ("Select a block before copying it."), Plan with nothing to
  place, a commitment over planned homework ("Find a new time"), a reminder, and
  School hours saved for every week did. Its Undo button reads, for example,
  Undo 8 while a thin line along the bottom shrinks as time runs out. Animations
  Off or Reduce removes the moving line but keeps the count. A screen reader
  reads the notice once. Its clock waits while the window is hidden, minimised
  or not in front, or while the pointer or keyboard is on it.
- Plan's question about overdue or left-over homework is a Material question.
  Paste and Duplicate show a Material preview where you can untick a row or
  change its day, start or minutes before choosing Paste or Duplicate. The line
  above says "Nothing changes until you choose Paste." Save on the recovery
  codes page now asks what the file is in a Material question.
- Replacing a running focus timer asks Keep current timer or Replace timer in a
  Material question. A choice that cannot be undone is marked red only when
  picked, and only then is the confirm button red. Delete, This week and later,
  Replace week, Restore, Discard and Sign out use the error colour in every
  question and sheet, in light and dark. Long notices wrap inside the window,
  and the keyboard returns to the screen after Plan or Undo.
- In My day's Up next and Done lists, free time now shows a sofa instead of the
  clock used by exercise and activities. The recovery codes page says "Turn on
  “I have saved these codes” to continue." under the switch.

### Phone
- The phone app has a new look matching the computer's Material style: the same
  Blue colours in light and dark and real icons. Today is an agenda with an Up
  next card, Add homework is one form, Week lists each session's time with
  arrows for other weeks, and Settings is grouped as This phone, Your computer
  and Help.
- Ticking a session marks that session done for that day only; the homework's
  other sessions stay planned. The widget tick does the same. The Week tab's
  homework total and heavy tag count only time still planned, not finished
  sessions.
- A session ending at midnight no longer crashes Today, the widget or Running
  late, and shows as 12 AM. Ending focus early counts the whole minutes worked
  toward the homework. A timer saved for deleted homework is cleared when the
  app opens instead of stopping every launch. Pausing or finishing focus from
  the notification updates the open Today card, and a paused timer is never
  counted as a full session.
- Plan, Accept, Running late's Move it, Start focus and Sync now now explain why
  they cannot act: nothing to place, the week changed on another device, the
  homework is gone, or sync failed. They no longer show an empty Placed 0 of 0
  sheet or do nothing.
- Running late names the homework, says when it now ends, and shows each moved
  session as from and to, such as 4 PM → 4:30 PM. Add homework now says Save
  puts it in No time yet and Plan gives it a time instead of saying FlexWeek
  picks the time.
- Settings no longer says No computer linked while Your computer shows a paired
  computer. A computer that is off says Your computer isn't reachable with the
  last sync time. Reachable replaces on the same Wi-Fi, which the phone cannot
  know.
- A failed sync or pairing says whether the computer is off or not reachable,
  needs an update, or had another problem. That code didn't match appears only
  for a wrong code.
- A phone edit made while the computer cannot be reached keeps the time the edit
  was made, not the next sync's time. An older edit can no longer undo a newer
  computer change or bring back deleted homework. A computer change the phone
  could not use because the app was old or its clock was far off is tried again
  on each sync, on both devices. The phone's sync line says how many changes are
  waiting and what to do; changes no update or clock fix can help are not
  counted.
- Week refreshes after every sync and rolls over to the new week on Monday.
  Today follows the real date when a new day starts, and the jump back to Today
  appears when you are on the same weekday of another week. Add homework on a
  Thursday or Sunday no longer shows a second Pick a day chip that moved the due
  date to Monday.
- The notification banner asks Android whether notifications are allowed. It
  goes away when they are and returns a few days after Not now if they are still
  off. Allow opens Android's own switch once Android will not ask again. On-time
  reminders reads on only when Android allows exact alarms and re-plans reminders
  when you allow them.
- Plan, each Running late slider step, saving homework and the Week tab no longer
  make the screen wait on the planner or a sync; they run in the background and
  show the result when ready. A sync after an edit no longer cancels one already
  running. Background syncs get the same 45 seconds as the computer, a computer
  that is off is asked less often over time, and sync after an edit can start on
  Android 11 and older.
- Back closes an open sheet, then goes to Today, and only then leaves the app.
  Rotating the phone keeps the tab, an open sheet and a half-typed Add homework;
  after Unpair it no longer brings the main screens back.
- A failed update check, such as one while offline, no longer counts as today's
  check. The next try waits an hour instead of 24 hours, and the updater no
  longer keeps the old screen after rotation. Settings no longer shows a Sound
  row with no control, and the sample reminder shows a real reminder's homework
  name, start and length.

### Fixed
- Ctrl+scroll zooms the Week and Day hours again on KDE Plasma's Wayland, where
  the mouse is reported as a touchpad.
- Rows in Paste and Duplicate previews and the Unfinished list have a thin edge
  like the choice cards. Duplicate's preview is titled Preview duplicate, and a
  pasted or duplicated row at a taken time says both blocks will show one
  slightly over the other.
- A reminder strip moves above a notice that grows to several lines, keeping Got
  it reachable. The notice's Undo gives the keyboard back to its block. In the
  Edited weeks question, All weeks and From today on are red when picked.
- The Terminal preset's Undo text now reads at 4.6 to 1, and the dark theme's
  Ctrl+Z hint is easier to read. In questions and sheets, the title, words,
  choice cards and fields start at the same left edge.
- Week as a list says each block's length and Placed by Plan or Fixed time. It
  also says when a block is overdue (with its due day), left from last week, or
  pinned. Unfinished has Start focus on overdue and left-over homework.
- Changing text size keeps the same time at the top of Week instead of scrolling
  back to now. The keyboard stays on the week after Plan, Undo, redo and dropping
  a block, on the block you acted on when it is still there. The notice's Undo is
  still a Tab away.
- Material's overdue row and its words update as soon as a deadline passes. My
  day's card and ring count homework due tonight that has no time yet and name
  it instead of calling the day free.
- Animations and Motion speed now change what moves. Off has no animation,
  Reduce keeps short fades and moves nothing, and More is slower and travels
  further than Normal. Look > Advanced > Motion speed (Slower, Normal, Faster)
  stretches or shortens every duration. Before, More moved like Normal, Off like
  Reduce, and the speed was saved but did nothing.
- Dialog buttons that do not fit side by side stack one per line instead of
  running outside the dialog. Copies of this week shows how many weeks each copy
  holds, including 1 week for one, instead of 0 weeks.

The assistant helper is still in the Linux downloads. Start it with
`./FlexWeek-x86_64.AppImage --mcp --read-only`, or `./FlexWeek --mcp --read-only`
from the extracted tarball. Drop `--read-only` to let it change your schedule.

## Known limits

- Windows and Android do not include the assistant helper.
- Developer builds are a Linux tarball only.
- The Android app does not have Month or Day screens.
- Sync uses the same Wi-Fi network or the public n0 relay and needs FlexWeek
  open on the computer.
- Material can use more memory over a long session. If FlexWeek grows slow, close
  and open it again.
- A developer build with a week that does not start on Monday saved some homework
  sessions at double length. They stay that long: shorten or delete them by hand.
- Weekly fixed times and their end dates need the 1.5.0 phone app. An older phone
  shows a weekly fixed time in every week, also after its end date, and if it
  saves such a week, that week keeps the time on every device. Update the phone
  app to 1.5.0.
