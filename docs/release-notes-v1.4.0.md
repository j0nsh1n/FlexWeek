# FlexWeek 1.4.0 release notes

Places homework around school and sports. Download, open a window, create an account.

Already using FlexWeek? Choose Check for updates in Settings, or use Update now
when the app offers it. Your account, schedule, saved looks, and paired devices
stay in place.

## What changed

### Start your week on any day
- The week can start on any of the seven days. Pick it in Settings (Widgets or
  Material) or in Setup, from a drop-down list. It is the same setting
  everywhere, and it is still kept on this computer. Week, Month, Day's small
  month, the date pickers, the study hours strip and the dial's row of days all
  start on it, and so do the day buttons for a block, School, activities,
  homework hours, routines and alarms.
- Week shows seven days from the day your week starts on. From Wednesday it runs
  Wednesday to Tuesday. Previous and Next move a week at a time, and Today opens
  the week that holds today. Sheets, notices and the day buttons name the days
  the same way, and a new first day shows at once on screens that are already
  open.
- Plan my homework, Replan all and Find a time plan the week you are looking at:
  each day uses its own weekday's work and study hours and protected time, Keep
  weekends homework-free keeps Saturday and Sunday free wherever they fall, and
  homework added with no due date is due at 11:59 PM on the week's last day.
- When a new first day moves a day you ticked to another date, the move is easy
  to spot: a ring on the day in the Widgets styles, a dot in Material, and a line
  such as "Monday 2 isn't in this week any more, so it moved to Monday 9." When
  it crosses into another month the line names both months. A screen reader says
  the line when it appears or changes.
- A sheet you closed with Esc and open again after the week start changed keeps
  only the days you ticked, each on its weekday.
- In the Widgets designs Classic, Timeline, Bento and Retro, an empty Saturday
  and Sunday take less of the Week's width wherever they fall.
- Setup and Settings no longer say "The Week view always runs Monday to Sunday."
- Plan my homework and Replan all no longer give a homework twice its time when
  the week starts on another day than Monday. A 1-hour homework now gets 1 hour,
  on screen and as saved.
- Homework you add today in a week that does not start on Monday no longer shows
  as "left from last week", and Plan my homework no longer asks "Before planning"
  about it. A session that passed unfinished before the week on screen still
  shows as left from last week.
- The notice after you add, move or place a block names the day from the week on
  screen: in a week that starts on Wednesday, dropping Science poster on Friday
  says "Placed Science poster on Fri 4:30 PM", not "Wed".

### Planner and homework time
- Replan all my homework now moves a homework session whose time has passed and
  that you did not finish. It gets a new time from now on, or no time (counted in
  "still needs a time") when there is no room before it is due. A session you are
  in the middle of, one you pinned by hand and a finished one stay where they are,
  and nothing is placed in the past.
- Plan my homework does the same for a session of this week whose time has passed
  and that you did not finish, with the same notice. A pinned session, a finished
  one, one on now and one later today stay where they are, and every other planned
  time stays. The assistant helper's Plan does the same, and Help and the helper's
  Plan description say so. If no room is left before it is due, it is left with no
  time rather than put in the past.
- A session you pinned by hand that passed unfinished stays where you put it, and
  its minutes still count as needing a time: Plan gives them a new session. In a
  week that is over it reads "History essay · 1 h pinned, in the past".
- Plan into this week now moves a homework session planned for a time that has
  passed and not finished (and not pinned), when "left from last week" counts its
  minutes. The Plan into this week button on a single homework in the Overdue and
  left from last week panel now gives a session that is waiting for a time its
  time.
- A session whose time passed without being finished is no longer shown as done.
  Day's dial tags it "Missed", Material Week no longer dims it, and the Material
  homework list says how long still needs a time.
- In a week that is over, a missed session is named as missed: "History essay · 1 h
  missed" in the homework lists and "1 h missed" in the status line. Use "Plan into
  this week" to give it a time this week.
- Plan my homework says what it did. A homework with minutes that do not fill a
  quarter hour is no longer short-changed without a word: the notice says, for
  example, "Placed 1 homework block. History essay still has 10 min with no time."
  in the plain look, and "Placed 1 of 1. History essay still has 10 min with no
  time." in Material. The Material drawer lists those minutes, and the count no
  longer shows a wrong line for a moment after a change is saved.
  The count is also right straight after a session is moved into or out of
  another week.
- The notice after Plan no longer says "1 with no time yet" or "Placed 1 of 2" for
  homework that was just given a time. When Plan finds nothing to place, the line
  counts a leftover under 15 minutes ("10 min still needs a time") instead of
  saying "Everything has a time."
- Time you have done now counts everywhere FlexWeek works out what a homework
  still needs: "left from last week", Plan, Day's due-soon list and next step,
  Month's deadlines, the homework list and Spread over days, and through the
  assistant helper. Done time is whichever is more, the focus timer
  minutes or the minutes of sessions you ticked done, never both added together.
  A 2-hour homework with an hour ticked done needs 1 hour, not 2.
- "Left from last week" no longer lists homework whose time is already planned.
  It shows only the minutes that still have no time.
- Homework past its deadline with fewer than 15 minutes left now stays in Overdue
  and Left from last week, and a leftover of 20 minutes is listed as 20, not 15.
  Pasting or duplicating a homework session with less than a quarter hour left
  now says so in minutes.
- After Allow splitting, if the plan cannot reach FlexWeek or a save fails, the
  next Plan still gives every session Allow splitting made a time this week where
  they fit.
- Spread over days gives the minutes a homework still needs a day with no session
  of it first. A session that already has a time no longer gets a second share on
  its day while another day before the due date is free.
- A homework saved In one go is no longer cut into two sessions by Plan when Split
  long tasks is on. That setting splits only long tasks that are not homework.
- Opening next week after planning this one no longer lists homework as "1 h left"
  or says "3 h still needs a time" when every minute already has a time later this
  week. A planned session holds its time while that time is still ahead; only one
  that passed unfinished needs a new time.
- Undoing a homework delete no longer loses minutes when you had shortened its
  session: a 60-minute essay with a session cut to 5 minutes, deleted and brought
  back, shows 55 minutes with no time again, not 0.
- Ending a focus session early now keeps the minutes you had worked. They count
  toward the homework instead of being lost. Nothing is marked finished by ending
  early.
- Add fixed time and Edit fixed time accept a start that has already passed, today
  or on an earlier day of the week, in both looks, and a fixed time can be dragged
  to an earlier time. The assistant helper follows the same rule. Homework still
  cannot be planned or pinned into the past.

### School and activities
- School hours shows your usual School (the days and times of every week), not the
  week you have open, with a line such as "This week: no School on Wednesday."
  when the open week has its own change. Run setup again does the same in both
  looks.
- When you change or delete School or an activity from this week on and a later
  week has its own change, FlexWeek asks first, in both looks. All weeks replaces
  that change. Except edited, the default, leaves it. From today on leaves the
  week that holds today and every earlier week as they are. Cancel saves nothing.
  When a change is saved without asking, a notice says which week kept its own
  change, for example "School changed from this week on. The week of 16 Nov keeps
  its own change: no School on Wednesday."
- Dragging or resizing one day of School or an activity, or Move to day... on one
  in Material, changes that day only, and its notice now says so: "Only this
  Wednesday changed. To change every week, open School hours."
- Skipping School on one day and then changing School hours no longer removes that
  day from every later week, and a change to School hours no longer turns a
  one-week name ("School trip") or start into your usual School.
- A day you deleted or moved in a later week no longer comes back, or shows twice,
  after School hours or Setup change School from an earlier week.
- An activity you ended "this week and later" stays ended when its time is changed
  from an earlier week.
- Running setup again in a week where you deleted School for that week only no
  longer brings it back that week.
- Removing an activity in Setup no longer gives the activity after it the removed
  one's number, so each activity keeps its own skipped days in later weeks.

### Dragging step
- Material Settings > Planning has a Dragging step choice, 5 min or 15 min. It is
  the same setting as Dragging in Widgets Settings and the planning page of Setup.
- Clicking or drawing on free time in Week and Day, and the time lists in Add and
  Edit fixed time and Add and Edit homework, now follow your Dragging step in
  Material and in the Widgets styles.
- An account that never chose a Dragging step now uses 15 minutes, new accounts
  included. An account already on 5 minutes moves to 15 once with this update;
  pick 5 again in Settings > Planning and it stays.
- When a homework time has already passed, the message names the next time on your
  step: on 5 minutes at 7:02 PM it says "Choose 7:05 PM or later today".
- Edit fixed time, and the Pinned time in Edit homework, show a time that is not
  on a quarter hour (for example 4:55 PM from a 5-minute drag) instead of a blank
  field.

### Material
- A block's menu in Week and Day (right-click, Shift+F10, the Menu key) has
  Duplicate, Move to day... and Copy, for fixed times and homework. Move to day...
  lists the week's days with today marked and has Undo. Ctrl+V pastes what Copy
  took.
- Week and Day blocks can be dragged: move a block to another time (and, in Week,
  another day), or drag its top or bottom edge to change its start or end. It
  shows the Undo notice, and Esc during the drag puts the block back and saves
  nothing.
- A right-click on free time in Week and Day opens the same menu a click does, and
  that menu has "Paste here" while something is copied.
- A fixed block has a menu with Edit and Delete on right-click, Shift+F10 and the
  Menu key, in Week and Day.
- Week and Day zoom in two levels closer, to 192 and 256 px an hour. A zoom you
  saved keeps its size.
- Month entries have the same coloured left strip a block or homework has in Week
  and Day.
- Clicking the Week or Month title opens a small month to go to a date. It works
  from the keyboard too.
- Esc on a new Add fixed time or Add homework sheet with something typed closes it
  at once, and the next Add of the same kind opens with what you typed.
- Pressing and dragging the mouse no longer pans the Day and Week grid, so it
  cannot fight dragging across free time to add something.
- Week and Day show the current time in the hour column at the now line's height,
  and a screen reader says "Current time, 12:17 PM".
- The now line in Week runs through today's column only and is drawn over the
  blocks. Today brings it back into view, and it moves to the current time when
  the app comes back to the front. Day draws it the same way.
- Material's P, Z and F keys have their own switch, Letter keys, in Settings >
  This computer, and it starts off. With Letter keys on, T goes to today in the
  view you are on.
- The first menu on a block opened near the bottom of the window no longer runs off
  the bottom edge. Tapping a row of an open list that hangs outside a sheet picks
  it instead of asking "Discard your changes?".
- The Duplicate and paste preview that opens over Material takes the keyboard: Esc
  closes it and adds nothing, and the number keys no longer change the screen
  behind it.
- Settings > Planning starts with Your week: Week starts on.
- The Plan my homework button is on whenever Plan can place something: a session
  waiting for a time, a session of this week that passed unfinished, or minutes no
  session holds that make a whole quarter hour. It stays off when only a few
  minutes under a quarter hour are left.
- "Add fixed time here" and "Add homework here" on free time in Week and Day open
  with the words kept by an earlier Esc, the same as the + button does, on the day
  and time you clicked.
- An open drop-down list (Week starts on in Settings, and the date and time lists
  in Add and Edit homework and fixed time) moves with Up and Down and picks with
  Enter.
- The number beside "No time yet" in the homework drawer counts every line the
  drawer lists, including homework with minutes that have no session.
- Week has a line above the grid when homework is overdue or left from last week,
  saying "1 overdue · 1 left from last week" as the Widgets styles do, with Plan
  into this week and Mark as finished. It is not there when nothing is left over.

### Keys and Focus (both looks)
- One key set in both looks. The plain look has the same two switches as Material,
  Number keys (on) and Letter keys (off), in Settings > This computer. With Letter
  keys on, T goes to today, D, W and M open Day, Week and Month, J and K step to
  the next and previous week, day or month, C adds homework, and P, Z and F plan,
  undo and open Focus. In the plain look D, W, M, T and F no longer work without
  Letter keys. Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Shift+Z, Ctrl+Y and the zoom keys work whatever either
  switch says.
- Ctrl+Shift+F starts Quick focus in both looks.
- Quick focus offers your homework: when a homework is on now, or is the next one
  today, the screen names it and the session counts toward it, with a Just a timer
  choice beside Start.
- Help opened from Material works from the keyboard: Esc closes it, Tab reaches the
  Close button, and Material's keys no longer change the screen behind it. Its key
  list shows the keys Material answers to.

### Widgets styles
- Esc on a new Add fixed time or Add homework sheet with something typed or picked
  closes it at once, and the next Add opens with it, as Material does. Cancel and
  the close button still ask "Discard what you typed?". The cursor goes after kept
  words, so typing carries on.
- A new Add fixed time sheet says its length ("1 h") under the times again; it
  could say "End moved to 8:15 PM to keep it 1 h." although nothing had been moved.
- The week notices time passing by itself: when a day ends, the header marks the
  new day as today, and when a planned session's time passes unfinished, the
  homework list stops saying "Everything has a time." without you pressing anything.

### Assistant helper
- It checks your "Week starts on" setting on every tool call, so changing it in
  Settings reaches a running helper without restarting it (`--week-starts-on`
  still overrides it). It names `week_starts_on` on every reply that names a week
  and takes the week as shown in its block, completion and `plan_homework` tools.
- It keeps the right time while it stays open: once your session runs out it
  refuses the next call, "today" follows the date, and past-time checks and Plan
  use the current time.
- Deleting School or an activity "from this week on" also removes a later week's own
  changed copy of it, as All weeks does in the app.
- Homework you add or edit with "spread over days" now works as the Spread over
  days choice in the app: it is cut into sessions of an hour (or what is left) on
  different days up to the due date, and Plan places each one on its day.

### Fixes
- With the week starting on Sunday, Month showed nothing on a date of its first row
  before that row's Monday (Sunday 29 November in December 2026). It now shows that
  date's homework and blocks, whatever the first day.
- With the week starting on a day other than Monday, FlexWeek no longer reloads the
  week again and again as if another program had changed it.
- Homework you delete while another week is open no longer comes back when you return
  to a week with unsaved changes.

The assistant helper is still in the Linux downloads. Start it with
`./FlexWeek-x86_64.AppImage --mcp --read-only`, or `./FlexWeek --mcp --read-only`
from the extracted tarball. Drop `--read-only` to let it change your schedule.

## Known limits

- Windows and Android do not include the assistant helper.
- Developer builds are a Linux tarball only.
- There is no new Android app in 1.4.0: the phone stays on 1.3.0 and keeps
  syncing with 1.4.0 on the computer. The phone's fixes and new look come in the
  next update.
- The Android app does not have Month or Day screens.
- Material is an added style. Today's app is still the default.
- Sync uses the same Wi-Fi network or the public n0 relay and needs FlexWeek
  open on the computer.
- In Material, Ctrl+mouse-wheel zoom may not work on some Linux desktops. Use Ctrl+=
  and Ctrl+- or the zoom buttons above the hours instead.
- Material can use more memory over a long session. If FlexWeek grows slow, close
  and open it again.
- A developer build with a week that does not start on Monday saved some homework
  sessions at double length. They stay that long: shorten or delete them by hand.
